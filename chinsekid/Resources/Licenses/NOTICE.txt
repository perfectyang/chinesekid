Stroke-order graphics

The bundled StrokeOrders.sqlite file contains stroke graphics and median paths
derived from the Make Me a Hanzi project's graphics.txt data:
https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi

The source graphics are derived from Arphic PL KaitiM GB and Arphic PL UKai
fonts and are distributed under the Arphic Public License. The data has been
converted to a compressed SQLite format by scripts/build-stroke-order-db.py.
The original license text is included as ARPHICPL.TXT. The upstream COPYING
notice is included as MakeMeAHanzi-COPYING.txt.

Pronunciation data in the same database comes from Make Me a Hanzi's
dictionary.txt, which is derived from Unihan and CJKlib. Its LGPL license text
is included as LGPL.txt; refer to MakeMeAHanzi-COPYING.txt for source details.
